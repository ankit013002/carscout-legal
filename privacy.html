Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Scout: Car Buying Agents ("Scout", "we", "us") is operated by Petalform LLC. Scout is a mobile marketplace that connects people shopping for a vehicle ("buyers") with independent car-buying agents ("agents"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Account information. Your name, email address and, if you choose to provide it, your phone number. If you sign in with Apple or Google we receive your name and email from that provider. We do not use passwords; email sign-in uses one-time codes.

Approximate location. The ZIP code you enter, used to match Hunts and agents by area. We do not collect precise device location.

Content you create. Car Hunt requirements, proposals, messages, candidate vehicles, negotiation updates, reviews, reports and the photos or documents you attach.

Agent application information. For agents: legal name, experience, specialties, service area, optional business and license details, and links you provide.

Payment information. Service payments are processed by Stripe. Card details are entered into Stripe's secure payment form and are never stored by Scout. We keep records of the service fees you paid or earned, refunds and payout status. Agent identity verification and payouts are handled by Stripe Connect.

Diagnostics and usage. Crash reports and performance data, and limited in-app activity events (for example "Hunt posted") that help us improve the product. These are tied to your account ID, not used for advertising, and never sold.

Device information. A push notification token so we can send you updates, if you allow notifications.

How we use information

  • To operate the marketplace: create and match Hunts, deliver proposals, messages and vehicle candidates, and process service payments, refunds and agent payouts.
  • To keep people safe: verify and review agents, detect fraud and off-platform payment requests, handle reports and disputes, and enforce our Terms.
  • To send notifications you request and important account or transaction messages.
  • To diagnose problems and improve Scout.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

What other users can see

  • Agents see your Hunt details, your first name and last initial, and your ZIP code and search radius. They do not see your email, phone number or address.
  • The agent you hire and you can see each other's messages and shared files within that Hunt.
  • Approved agents' public profiles (display name, photo, bio, specialties, service area, ratings and published reviews) are visible to buyers.
  • Your written review and star rating of an agent are public with your first name and last initial.

Service providers

We share information only with providers that help us run Scout, under contracts that limit their use of it: Supabase (hosting and database), Stripe (payments, identity verification and payouts), Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging / Apple Push Notification service (push notifications), and, if enabled, Sentry (crash reporting) and PostHog (product analytics). We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety and rights of our users or others.

Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. If you delete your account we remove or anonymize your personal information. Some records are kept in de-identified form, or as long as needed for legal, tax, accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes — for example transaction and payout records and the history of Hunts involving another user.

Your choices and rights

  • Update your profile at any time in the app under Profile.
  • Turn push notifications off in the app or in your device settings.
  • Delete your account in the app (Profile → Account & privacy → Delete account) or as described on our account deletion page.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of your personal information. Contact us to make a request; we will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to your data is enforced on our servers so that only the people involved in a Hunt, and authorized Scout staff for support and safety, can see it. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique email account and keep your device secure.

Children

Scout is intended for adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy. If we make material changes we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.
