Delete your Scout account

Scout: Car Buying Agents · Petalform LLC

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Scout and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Account & privacy.
  3. Type DELETE and tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

By email

If you can't access the app, email petalformllc@gmail.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Scout account". We will confirm and complete the request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email, phone number, ZIP code and profile photo
  • Your sign-in identity, push notification tokens and blocked-user list
  • Draft Hunts; open Hunts are cancelled
  • For agents: your public profile details, application details and private notes

What is kept, and why

To protect the other person in a transaction and meet legal, tax and fraud-prevention obligations, we keep de-identified records of completed Hunts, messages already sent to the other party, payment, refund and payout records, reviews, reports and audit logs. These are no longer linked to your name or contact details.

Before you delete

Active Hunts must be completed or cancelled, and agents must receive any pending payouts, before the account can be deleted. The app will tell you if anything is outstanding.
