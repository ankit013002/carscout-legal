Common questions

What am I paying for? A fixed fee for your agent's search, research and negotiation help. It is not a payment toward any vehicle.

Who do I pay for the car? The dealer or seller, directly. Scout and agents never receive vehicle purchase money.

Can I get a refund? Yes if your agent cancels, or if you cancel early before any vehicles are sent. Later cancellations are reviewed by support. See the Terms.

Does Scout guarantee the car? No. Get an independent inspection for used vehicles and verify the title.

Stay safe

Never send gift cards, cryptocurrency, wires or deposits to an agent. Report anyone who asks — long-press a message in the app to report it.

Becoming an agent

Choose "I want to be an agent" when you sign up (or Profile → Become an agent). Every application is reviewed before you can send proposals.
